# Privacy summary (installer)

- Your work stays on this PC by default (`data/` folder).
- Nothing is sent to Netie cloud unless you connect a tunnel, BYOK key, or Netie account login.
- We do not train AI models on your content.
- AirGPT installer can include Netie Space; one Netie account links both.
- Optional keyboard shortcuts (Copilot key / Win+Space) use a minimal hook — no keystroke logging.
