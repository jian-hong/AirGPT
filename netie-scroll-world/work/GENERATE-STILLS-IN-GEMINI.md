## Rules that make or break the world

1. **Landscape 3:2 aspect** (e.g. 1536×1024 or larger). Ask Gemini for "wide 3:2
   landscape, high resolution" if it gives you a square.
2. **The first paragraph of every prompt must stay byte-identical** — that shared
   style preamble is what makes six images read as one world. Don't paraphrase it.
3. **Background must be plain, solid, near-black (#050505)** — the page background is
   that color, so the scenes blend seamlessly. If Gemini adds a gradient sky or vignette,
   regenerate: "plain solid uniform #050505 background, no gradient".
4. **No text anywhere in the image.** If letters/numbers sneak in, regenerate.
5. **Keep the subject centered with a little headroom** — the camera dives toward the
   center, and phones crop the sides.
6. When you have all six, put them side by side and squint: same angle, same palette,
   same lighting? Any odd one out — regenerate just that one, mentioning "same style as
   the previous images".
